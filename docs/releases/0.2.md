## Beiwagen 0.2

The first Beiwagen release: the FM-1 as a USB sidecar for the teenage engineering OP-1 Field. One USB cable; the OP-1
is the host.

**Tested on an FM-1 with an OP-1 Field (1.7.3):**

- **OP-1 → FM-1:** in USB mode 8 the OP-1's four tape tracks arrive as four stereo pairs, 44.1 kHz.
- **FM-1 → OP-1:** the chosen pair plays on the FM-1 and goes back as the OP-1's USB input, to record onto another track.
- **Keys → OP-1:** the FM-1's keys play the OP-1 over USB-MIDI (set the OP-1's MIDI to receive notes).

**Built, not yet tested on an FM-1:** the mixdown (GLO: four tracks with inserts, delay and reverb sends; REC finds the
return track), the sequencer and arpeggiator (SEQ, ARP), presets and patterns on the keys (hold SAVE or SEL + key).

Installs over M-VAVE's V15 and over other community firmware, and back.
